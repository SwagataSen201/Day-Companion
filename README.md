# Day Companion 🐱

> A calm, mobile-first day companion designed to help people navigate time,
> activities, and transitions with less mental effort.

---

## Overview

Day Companion is an early-stage productivity prototype exploring how a
simpler, transition-focused experience could help people navigate their day.

Instead of functioning like a traditional to-do list or productivity
dashboard, Day Companion focuses on helping users understand:

- What am I doing now?
- How much time is left?
- What happens next?
- How do I get ready for the next activity?

The product is designed around a simple principle:

> **Reduce the mental effort required to manage the day.**

---

## The Problem

People may know what they need to do but still:

- Lose track of time
- Drift into unplanned activities
- Struggle to start important tasks
- Forget routine activities
- Find it difficult to transition from one activity to another

The product particularly explores the gap between:

**"I know what I should do"**

and

**"I actually move from what I'm doing now to what I need to do next."**

Day Companion focuses on this transition problem rather than simply tracking
whether a task was completed.

> **Important:** Day Companion is a productivity/support prototype. It is not
> a medical application and does not diagnose, treat, or cure ADHD or any
> other medical condition.

---

## Product Hypothesis

> **If users can clearly see what they should be doing now, what comes next,
> how much time remains, and receive a gentle transition cue, they may find it
> easier to stay on track.**

This is a product hypothesis that will be evaluated through user testing.
It is not yet a validated conclusion.

---

## Core Product Principle

> **Reduce the mental effort required to manage the day.**

Every major interaction is designed around one question:

> **"What does the user need to know or do right now?"**

---

## Core Experience

```text
Prepare Tomorrow
       ↓
   Open App
       ↓
   Cat Welcome
       ↓
      NOW
       ↓
      NEXT
       ↓
Transition Reminder
       ↓
    I'm Ready
       ↓
    Time's Up
       ↓
Complete / Continue / Skip
       ↓
      +2 ⭐
       ↓
 Next Activity
```
